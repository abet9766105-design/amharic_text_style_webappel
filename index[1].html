How it works

Amharic is written in the Ge'ez (Ethiopic) script, and many online text tools cannot draw it properly. This tool renders each Amharic character with an Ethiopic font and then applies visual effects such as glowing neon tubes, polished metal, flames, galaxy backgrounds, pencil sketches and more. The result is a square 900 x 900 PNG image that you can save or share.

  1. Type a short word or phrase, for example a name, a greeting or a holiday wish.
  2. Optionally add your own photo to use as the background.
  3. Pick a style group, choose a style, and press Create image.
  4. Press Download PNG, or press-and-hold the image on your phone to save it.

Ideas for using your images

  • Greeting cards for holidays such as Enkutatash, Meskel, Timkat, Eid and Christmas.
  • Profile pictures, channel banners and social media posts.
  • Name designs for birthdays, weddings and invitations.
  • Posters for school events, shops and small businesses.

Tips for the best results

  • Short text looks best. One to three words fill the picture with large, clear letters.
  • Dark photos work well behind bright neon and fire styles; bright photos suit pencil and ink styles.
  • If a style looks too busy, try another one. Each style handles letter shapes differently.

Frequently asked questions

Is it free?

Yes. The generator is free to use and does not need an account.

Are my text and photos saved?

No. Your text and photo are processed in memory to create the image and are not stored on the server. See the Privacy Policy.

Why does the first image take a little longer?

The server may be waking up after a quiet period. Later images usually appear within a few seconds.

What file do I get?

A 900 x 900 pixel PNG image.

Can I type English letters or numbers too?

Yes, but the tool is designed for Amharic and other Ge'ez script text, so Amharic gives the best look.
