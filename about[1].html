About {{ site_name }}

{{ site_name }} is a free online tool that turns Amharic (Ge'ez script) text into decorative images. It started as a Telegram bot and is now available as a website, so anyone with a phone or computer can use it without installing anything.

Why we built it

Many design tools and fonts do not support the Ethiopic script well. Writing a name or a holiday greeting in Amharic with a glowing neon, golden or fire effect usually means a long manual process. This tool makes that quick: type, choose a style, download.

How it works

The text is drawn with an Ethiopic font and then processed with mathematical effects (lighting, textures, gradients and noise) to create each style. There are more than fifty styles in five groups: Light & Neon, Metal & Stone, Fire & Nature, Sky & Space, and Art & Drawing.

Your privacy

Text and photos you submit are used only to create your image and are not stored. Read the full Privacy Policy.
